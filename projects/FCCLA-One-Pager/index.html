What we’re here for

Morrow High School FCCLA empowers students to become leaders, strengthen families, explore careers, serve their community, and create meaningful connections through leadership and service. Everyone has a place here.

Why join us?

  • Lead with more confidence.
  • Connect with friends and professionals.
  • Serve Morrow.
  • Create projects that matter.
  • Compete in STAR Events when you’re ready.
